Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect your Telegram User ID and Username to manage your account and subscription status. If you connect a TON wallet, we may store your public wallet address to verify premium access.

Your data is used solely for the functionality of the bot (providing signals, managing subscriptions, and tracking usage limits). We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Security

We implement standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services

We interact with the Telegram API, Binance API (for charts), and TON Connect. Please review their respective privacy policies.

5. Your Rights

You can request the deletion of your data by contacting the bot administrator.
